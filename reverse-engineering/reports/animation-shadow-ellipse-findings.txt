record

Axis evaluation order is offsets6,3,9. Each squared length is float-stored before the root and its reciprocal is float-stored; zero length gives inverse0.

ring

Reconstruct offset in axis order3,6,9, float-store it, then add world center and float-store again. These separate stores matter and are absent from the simplified decompiler expression.
