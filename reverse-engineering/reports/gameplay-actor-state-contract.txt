range
SetSide range= uses the original0x5147c0 packet/numeric parser and float32 squared radius. A positive squared radius enters group traversal even without type filters. Candidate distances are full3D, float32 component differences and final squared sum in z,x,y order, with strict distanceSquared<radiusSquared. A type filter and a range filter combine conjunctively. A missing selected center leaves distance scratch0, so positive range can select every eligible actor of the requested types or all types. Negative radii square positive; zero range disables range filtering.
