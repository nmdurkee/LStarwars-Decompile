composition

ordering
Original5f4ff0 calls actual5da980 only when cached visibility is-1, then optional secondary visibility. If both invisible it clears the layer submission flag and leaves the queue unchanged. Otherwise it temporarily sets cached visibility, queues shadows, reserves skin palettes and calls rigid/smooth draw services in original order; it restores the prior visibility and clears the layer submission flag on return.
