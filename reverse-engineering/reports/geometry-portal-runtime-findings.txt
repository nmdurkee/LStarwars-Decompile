Observed traversal rules
The ancestor loop tests path indices[0,depth-2],then rejects depth>=16. It writes
the current slot,marks root-room membership unconditionally or clipped child

portal list order is retained; previous-room links and inactive portals are

Projection/FOV math
Projection first stores homogeneous W and its reciprocal tofloat32,then
multiplies each still-wide coordinate dot product by that stored reciprocal.
W0 produces reciprocal0. Bounds clamp each coordinate to[-1,1]. The original
function also computes an inverse cache at0x97ad50; the recursive path consumes
the projection result,not that cache. The portable projection result omits this
side effect and does not claim a complete generic matrix-inverse reconstruction.

FOV construction stores half-FOV tofloat32,multiplies by the original exact
double10430.3779296875,then truncates. Original32768-entry sine bytes determine
tangent and aspect scaling. Each slope,squared length,sqrt,reciprocal and plane
component follows its observedfloat32 store. Shared integer PlaybackWide retains
64-significand intermediates where a prematurefloat32/double result is wrong.
The world camera/projection fixtures are prepared inputs; live camera ownership
and its view/projection assembly are not recovered by these comparisons.
